Origin: https://www.vb-cable.com/
VB-CABLE is donationware. If you find it useful, please donate/pay for your license:
https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

The original VB-CABLE Driver Pack 45 is included without modification.
The vendor's public "VB-CABLE Distribution with other product" grant permits
bundling with applications when VB-Audio's identity and donationware model
remain visible and applicable. VB-CABLE A+B and C+D are not included.
Professional deployments in companies, institutions, or organizations where
users cannot pay for their software require VB-Audio volume licensing.
VB-CABLE is not owned by STTS and remains subject to its original license.

Installing the virtual microphone requires administrator permission and a PC
restart. Existing VB-CABLE installations are reused. In Discord, select
"CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)" as the input device. Select your
headphones/speakers as Discord's output device. STTS sends generated speech
to "CABLE Input"; it never routes generated speech to physical speakers.

The STTS application/installer is an unsigned experimental release.
The bundled Windows 10/11 VB-CABLE driver is vendor-signed. STTS does not
change Secure Boot, driver-signing policy, or trusted certificate stores.
Uninstalling STTS preserves your saved voices/models and the shared VB-CABLE
driver. Remove saved data from %LOCALAPPDATA%\STTS if desired; remove VB-CABLE
with VB-Audio's own setup program before deleting its files.
